03 / How to play

Easy to learn.
Hard to stay un-IT.

  1. 1

    One player is IT

    Touch someone and they become IT. The player you just tagged is protected for a moment so you can't be tagged right back.

  2. 2

    Every second counts

    You earn a point for every second you are not IT. A round lasts 90 seconds, then the next arena loads and everybody resets.

  3. 3

    Use your power

    Time your ability. Cooldowns are long on purpose; a good dodge is worth more than a panicked one.
