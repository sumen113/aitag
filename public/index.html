01 / Characters

Eight fighters.
Eight ways to dodge.

Every character has one power on a cooldown. None is best — they trade speed, size and tricks. Learn yours, then learn how to beat the rest.

02 / Arenas

Five arenas.
New one every round.

Mud slows you, ice won't let you stop, boost pads fling you across the map, and teleporters drop you behind the tagger. Use the terrain.

03 / How to play

Easy to learn.
Hard to stay un-IT.

  1. 1

    One player is IT

    Touch someone and they become IT. The player you just tagged is protected for a moment so you can't be tagged right back.

  2. 2

    Every second counts

    You earn a point for every second you are not IT. A round lasts 90 seconds, then the next arena loads and everybody resets.

  3. 3

    Use your power

    Time your ability. Cooldowns are long on purpose; a good dodge is worth more than a panicked one.
